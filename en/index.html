Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2026

Thank you for using Petalance (the “App”).
The App is named “Petalance” in Japanese and “Petalance” in English.
This Privacy Policy explains how user information is handled in the App.

1Information You Provide

The App may process the following information entered by users:

  • Habit and activity names
  • Completion history
  • Customization preferences
  • App settings

This habit data is generally stored locally on your device.

2Information Collected Automatically

The App or its third-party services may collect the following information:

  • Device information and operating system version
  • App crash and performance information
  • Information required to display advertisements
  • Purchase status, including Premium entitlement status
  • An installation ID, product ID, and store transaction information used to verify purchases

3Advertising

The free version uses Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK to deliver advertisements. Google may use advertising identifiers, device information, and related data for ad delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention.

For more information, please review the Google Privacy Policy.

4In-App Purchases

Premium purchases and tips to the developer are processed through the Apple App Store or Google Play. The App does not obtain payment information such as your credit card number.

Purchase status and identifiers required for verification, restoration, refunds, and revocations may be transmitted to and stored on a server managed by the developer.

5Data Storage

Habit, history, customization, and settings data entered by users is generally stored locally on the device. Information required for purchase verification may be stored on cloud servers to manage Premium entitlements, prevent fraud, restore purchases, and process refunds or revocations.

6Notifications

If permitted by the user, the App may provide reminder notifications. Notification content and timing are managed on the device, and habit data is not transmitted to third parties for reminder delivery.

7Third-Party Services

The App may use the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK
  • Apple App Store / Apple In-App Purchase
  • Google Play / Google Play Billing
  • Google Cloud Firestore

Please review each provider’s privacy policy for details about how that provider handles information.

8Use by Minors

Minors should use the App with the consent of a parent or guardian where appropriate.

9Changes to This Policy

This Policy may be revised in response to changes in law, new App functionality, or changes to services used by the App. Material changes will be announced on this page or through another appropriate method.
